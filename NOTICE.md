# Provenance

Watch Lock by Daniel Moya (@danimoya, me@danimoya.com).

`Service.qml`, `LockView.qml`, and `FingerprintModel.js` derive from the installed
Omarchy 4.5.0-3 lock service, https://github.com/omacom/omarchy (MIT).
Upstream password/PAM, fingerprint pacing, sleep recovery and wallpaper support
retain their original attribution. Watch-mode state, coverage checks, the CLI,
hover fading, tests and preview fixtures are contributed by Daniel Moya.

External programs are not bundled. Omarchy, Arch Linux and other third-party
names do not imply endorsement. Preview app contents are invented test fixtures.
